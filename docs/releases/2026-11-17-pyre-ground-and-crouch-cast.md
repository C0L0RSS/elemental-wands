# Fire: Dragon's Pyre follows the ground, and crouching casts

- Dragon's Pyre always runs its full 40 blocks along the floor in the direction you
  face. Looking up at a boss no longer cuts it off after a few blocks.
- It follows the ground: down drops of up to three blocks, up one-block steps, across
  deeper holes, and from the edge of a ledge. Taller walls, fences, water and lava stop
  it, each strip of the five-wide wall on its own.
- It only hurts where its fire is, so an enemy behind a pillar is safe.
- For every wand, Sneak + right click now casts while you crouch in a fight. It still
  opens doors and chests, works the church sockets, the Storm gong and the Pyre seal, and
  trades with villagers or mounts a horse when you aim at them within reach. Toggle sneak
  and rebound keys count.
- Crouched with a shield or food in your off hand, Sneak + right click now casts instead
  of using it, unless you aim at something to interact with within reach.
