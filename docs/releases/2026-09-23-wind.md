# Wind: Gale Daggers and Updraft

- Prepare three pearl-white daggers overhead, then press again or use primary fire
  to launch a rapid volley. Aim each shot as it releases; each blade leaves a
  trail of small, fading cloud-white rings.
- The daggers reach 40 blocks and deal 5 base damage each. Their 12-second cooldown
  starts when fired; all three can hit the same enemy.
- Sky Shear now reaches seven blocks, keeping Wind's regular attacks close-range.
- Updraft launches you about ten blocks upward with normal horizontal air control
  and a safe landing. Use it on the ground or in midair; it recovers in eight seconds.
  An expanding ring of white smoke puffs spreads from your feet when you launch.
- Waylay Dash's base and chained boosts are reduced to 75% of their previous strength.
