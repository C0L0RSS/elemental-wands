# Co-op parties and allied wand protection — September 12, 2026

Players can form an invite-only party without operator permission. Party membership
is independent of Minecraft scoreboard teams: joining a party never replaces a
team, its name/color, or its scoreboard settings.

## Player commands

| Command | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `/party` or `/party list` | Members, leader, online/offline status, pending invitations, and help |
| `/party create` | Create a party and become its leader |
| `/party invite <player>` | Leader invites an online player; invitation expires after 60 seconds of server ticks |
| `/party accept [leader]` | Join only with a valid invitation; name the leader if several invitations are pending |
| `/party decline [leader]` | Decline an invitation |
| `/party leave` | Leave; leadership passes to the next remaining member if necessary |
| `/party kick <member>` | Leader removes an online or offline member |
| `/party transfer <member>` | Transfer leadership to another member, including an offline member |
| `/party disband` | Leader closes the party |

All commands also work under `/ew party`. There is one party per player, with up
to 32 members. Invites have a three-second sender cooldown and a recipient can
hold up to 16 pending invitations. Accepting requires consent and a free membership
slot. Transferring leadership/disbanding invalidates the previous leader's invites.
No invitation survives a server restart; accepted membership does.

For two players, one runs `/party create`, then `/party invite BrotherName`.
The other runs `/party accept`. `/party` shows the result.

## Combat contract

`party/WandAllies` is the shared server-side decision used before targeting,
projectile collision, damage, ignition, crowd control and explosion knockback.
The caster, party members, vanilla teammates, their owned pets, and their Nature
ultimate tree are protected. Existing Minecraft teammates receive wand protection
even if that scoreboard team's vanilla friendly-fire option is enabled.
The server's PvP-disabled game rule also suppresses indirect player harm.

Coverage includes the unbound wand's direct attack, all four traveling primary
projectiles, Stone Gathered Mass, Fire Flamethrower and Fire Hop, Pyre's front and
coals, Nature seedling/Bloom thorns and slows, Nature root crush, Stone wall shatter
and Titan's confinement pull, Hollow Purple touch damage, and Meteor/Zephyr
explosion damage and knockback. Allied projectiles pass through players instead
of being consumed by them. Space guidance and Nature tendrils skip allies.
Delayed contact resolves current membership using UUIDs; lingering effects still
respect parties and remembered vanilla team names if the caster disconnects or
moves to another dimension.

Temporary fire and Pyre coals now retain their caster in the existing temporary
block ownership record. Meteor-generated ground fire uses the existing
vanilla-looking Inferno flame, remains dangerous to opponents, lasts 60 seconds,
and does not spread. This deliberately prevents anonymous, spreading spell fire
from bypassing co-op protection. Non-wand explosions and natural fire retain
vanilla behavior. No textures or models changed.

This is a wand combat party, not territory claiming or a replacement for Minecraft's
scoreboard system. Ordinary sword/bow PvP follows Minecraft's existing rules.
Walls, domes and trees remain physical blocks, and spells' terrain changes remain
shared world changes. Joining a party prevents new allied hits; it does not cleanse
status effects already applied before joining. Caster-only buffs/healing retain
their current recipients. Bosses still attack the group and keep existing encounter
admission, damage and difficulty scaling.

## Persistence and integration

- `party/PartyStore`: versioned UUID membership and leader in
  `<world>/elementalwands/parties.json`. Validated immutable snapshots; atomic,
  forced-to-disk write before publishing any membership change.
- Corrupt party data is preserved and prevents unsafe startup; a failed command
  write leaves the live membership unchanged and logs a clear error.
- `party/PartyManager`: per-server state, transient invitations, names and cooldowns;
  server-stop cleanup prevents cross-world state leakage.
- `command/PartyCommands`: non-operator Brigadier commands and namespaced alias.
- `party/WandExplosionBehavior`: delegates existing damage tuning while excluding
  allied damage and knockback. `WandExplosionFireMixin` scopes owned ground fire
  strictly to these wand explosions.
- `TemporaryBlockManager` tracks optional caster UUIDs alongside placement ownership;
  replacement, expiry and restoration use the existing ownership lifecycle.
