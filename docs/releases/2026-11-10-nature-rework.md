# Nature: flowers that fight beside you, nothing in the way

Nature keeps its garden but stops tangling the fight in it. It now plays the same against a
boss as against anything else.

- **Sentry Bloom** replaces Tendril Bloom. Aim at the ground and plant a flower; it spits a
  small seed every two seconds at an enemy within 16 blocks. You keep three at a time, each
  for 24 seconds, on a 6-second cooldown.
- **Seed** and **Thornbite** aim your flowers. Whatever you hit is marked for five seconds, a
  seed turns above its head, and every flower of yours that can see it shoots it. With no mark
  your flowers pick the nearest monster and leave animals and other players alone.
- A flower's head turns to face what it is about to shoot, kicks back when it fires and hangs
  its head for its last two seconds.
- Each flower seed deals 2.5 damage, growing with your Nature level, and heals you for a fifth
  of the damage it deals.
- **Full Bloom** is the new ultimate, replacing Overgrowth and its oak. A harmless wave of
  petals rolls out ten blocks from you. You and every ally it reaches regenerate and gain two hearts of
  absorption for twelve seconds. For those twelve seconds your Seeds come faster, Thornbite
  heals more, flowers go down twice as fast, and every flower you own grows bigger, with a second head on a branch, so
  it spits twice as many seeds and can shoot two enemies at once. On grass the wave leaves
  grass and wild flowers behind.
- Flowers are scenery with a job: nothing can stand on one, trip on one, aim at one or break
  one, and they never slow, hold or lift anything. Full Bloom puts no block in a fight.
- Vines, bramble patches, root knots, thorn ticks and banked flower health are gone. Seed and
  Thornbite no longer slow or root.
- Bosses take Nature's damage like any other target. The Guardian no longer slams to clear
  your plants or gives up extra recovery to Nature, and nothing of Nature's lifts a boss off
  the floor.
- Springbloom's panel now shows its real 6-second cooldown and 8-second pad.
- Verdant Step is gone: holding the Nature wand no longer grows lily pads to walk on water.
- Nature's effects are now chunky 3D petals, leaves and pollen, in the same style as the Fire,
  Wind and Space wands. A heal shows as sap leaves and gold pollen spiralling up around you.

Not yet playtested in Lunar.
