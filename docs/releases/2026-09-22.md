# Nature: Thornbite and Springbloom

- Reworked Thorn Lash into Thornbite: a fast Venus flytrap bite on intertwined vines extending from the wand.
- The bite commits to your aim, hits one enemy, and retains its damage-based healing. Existing purchases and equipped slots carry over.
- The flytrap now launches and returns at the wand tip along the hand side, with a smaller jaw to keep the center of the view clearer.
- Matched Thornbite and its icon to Nature’s bright greens, pink/violet flowers, and golden accents.
- Added Springbloom, Nature’s fifth ability: throw a short-lived jump flower that anyone can use or break. Landing launches you high and protects your next landing, with a ten-second cooldown.
- Springbloom now plants through grass, ferns and leaf litter instead of requiring an entirely empty area.
- Springbloom can open beside obstacles as a smaller flower. Blocked sections lose both their visible petals and their landing surface.
