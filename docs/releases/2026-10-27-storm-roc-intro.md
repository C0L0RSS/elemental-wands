# Storm Roc: the snatch

- Calling the Storm Roc now plays its intro. Strike the gong twice and the storm closes in. A
  caw from above, then talons snap shut around you and the peak falls away. Look up and you
  see the Roc for the first time as it screams down at you, and then you're carried into the
  storm cloud.
- The cloud takes the place of the loading screen: it holds, lit by lightning, while the sky
  realm loads, and opens over the sea of clouds.
- Above the clouds you see yourself, in your own skin, clamped flat in its talon. You strike
  it with your wand in your element's colour, and it shrieks and flings you at the summit.
  You crash into the snow and roll to your feet facing its spire. It lands there under its
  name, and you get a long look at it before the view glides back into your eyes.
- The fight opens with the Roc summoning its first tornado. On its spire it rears up and
  screams a bolt into the clouds. The storm strikes the summit and a funnel reaches down out of
  the cloud as it beats the wind at it. Every new tornado now touches down this way rather than
  appearing whole, and does no harm until it has formed.
- Each player sees their own scene, and the fight starts once everyone has landed. Hold Sneak
  to skip it: a skip waits in the cloud until the summit has loaded, then hands back.
- While the scene plays you can't be hurt, move, cast or use anything. A player whose game falls
  too far behind is left at the gong and can try again a little later.
- Sounds are stand-ins for now, and your wand isn't in your hand during the struggle yet.
