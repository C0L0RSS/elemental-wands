# Nature: thrown flowers that store health

Follow-up changes to the Nature wand after the rework.

- **Sentry Bloom is thrown.** The cast lobs a bud in an arc, like Gravity Well's bomb, and the
  flower grows where it lands. Throw it at a wall and it takes the ground below; throw it at
  a monster and it takes root at its feet. The 6-second cooldown starts when you throw, and a
  bud that finds no ground is lost.
- **Flowers store health.** A flower's hits no longer heal you as they land. Each flower keeps
  a fifth of the health its seeds take, up to four hearts, and puffs hearts while it holds
  some. Walk up to your own flower (within three blocks), aim at it and right-click to collect:
  you are healed by what it stored and the flower is used up. A flower that wilts, is replaced
  or is broken takes its stored health with it.
- **Flowers can be broken.** Any player can aim at a flower's stem from within three blocks
  and break it with one hit: a left-click with a wand, or an ordinary punch without one.
  Flowers are still walk-through, and monsters still ignore them.
- **Seed flies clean.** Your own Seed no longer leaves a trail of pollen and leaves. A flower's
  small seed keeps its thin pollen trail.

Collecting and breaking follow your slot keys: slot 2 collects and slot 1 breaks, which are
right and left mouse unless you have rebound them.

Not yet playtested in Lunar.
