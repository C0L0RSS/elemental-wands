In Minecraft

Screenshots from the mod's scripted test fight on the summit, rendered by the game itself.

What you're seeing

This is the game's own model and animation data, baked frame by frame from the files the mod loads, and lit like a Minecraft mob. It is about 300 solid blocks, many of them angled, carrying a pixel-art texture; the eyes are lit by a glow layer, as in game.

The ticks on the timeline are server ticks, 20 a second. The blue marks are the moments the fight lands a hit, so you can check each blow against the motion.

Not shown here: the server moving the Roc (flights between spires, the dive's climb, the pounce arc), its head turning to track you, the blends between clips, the lightning and its crackle. Those only appear in game.

While its feet are down the tail lifts just enough to stay off the rock; on a spire it may hang over the drop.
