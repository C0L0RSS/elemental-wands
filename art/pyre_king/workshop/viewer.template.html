Leaning in with his head lowered, the masked king glares from under a crown of tapered gold wedges, one sheared off. The head sits in a stepped gorget; the burned mantle drapes sloped two-plate pauldrons. A long, tapering ash beard ends above the socketed shard, where a rust-iron curse chain hangs its padlock. Gauntleted fists grip a 4.5-block longsword whose whole blade burns upward; a short iron tether runs from the wrist shackle to the pommel. A flared, gold-edged coat to the knee, a crimson tabard and a long burnt cape.

Rig / material notes

Mask, crown, three curse bindings, nine mantle scraps, six coat flaps, wrist shackle and tether are independently rigged. The sword belongs to the right hand and also has a standalone export.

Geometry uses the Necromancer workshop’s GeckoLib 5 transforms and UVs. Fire has a separate alpha-blended pass; the sweep is a review exercise with stretching forearms, not a production combat clip or an in-game render.
