an implementation instruction. Agreed decisions belong in [Vision](Vision.md);
scheduled or selected work belongs in [Progress](Progress.md).

## New ideas

Write freely here. We can organize or discard a thought after discussing it.

## Questions to ask of an idea

- What would a player do with it?
- Does it improve builds, progression, exploration, SMP combat, or getting started?
- How does it interact with what already exists?
- What small experiment would show whether it is enjoyable?

## When we decide to use an idea

Move its agreed rules into Vision and add a stable work-item ID to Progress.
Keep the idea entry as a brief link, or remove it once it no longer helps.
