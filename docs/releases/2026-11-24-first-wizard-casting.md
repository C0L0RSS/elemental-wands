# The First Wizard casts with its tentacles

- The floating copy now has three slim, square-linked arms of black ink. A glint runs
  along the joints as a cast builds. All three twist into an overhead hammer for Slam
  or a low whip for Sweep. Watch for the hammer to stop turning, or jump the whip as
  it reaches you.
- Fireball draws the clone into the air with three arms flared beneath the flame. For
  Hollow Purple it hangs looking up between open tentacles while red and blue stars spiral
  together overhead. Their fusion signals the lock before the orb tips into its roll.
- The spells have painted 16-pixel textures. Look for pale wind cuts, hooked flames, leaf veins
  and cracked slate. Hollow Purple forms a solid violet orb with two broken rings around it.
- The other spells each have their own gesture. Sky Shear cuts across, down and back.
  Meteor braces on two limbs and slings the rock with the third. Thornbite snaps its jaws,
  Blink Rift pulls open a seam, and two tentacles pump the Flame nozzle in turn.
- After a cast, the shoulder recoils first and the tips unwind a beat later.
  When the boss chains spells, the next windup carries on from that pose.
- The clone throws its body into the cast. It arches under Slam, pivots through Sweep,
  and tucks its legs to catch a Leap. Before the transformation, its casting stance
  follows the hand that holds the wand.
- The wizard's floor warnings are gone in both phases. Watch its cast and the spell it
  sends. Falling cinders show where a Meteor is coming down, and debris spirals through
  the air toward an active Gravity Well.
