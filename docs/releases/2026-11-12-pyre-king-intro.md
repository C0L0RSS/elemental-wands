# Pyre King: a more natural awakening

- On his throne his right forearm now rests along the arm of the throne, the hand hanging
  over its end, and his left hand lies on his thigh.
- He reaches for the fallen sword by pulling himself forward first, then lifting his hand
  away, with his elbow kept low. It no longer swings across his body as he grabs the hilt.
- He gets up the way a heavy man would: leaning over his feet and pushing up on his knee and
  the sword. His left arm then hangs at his side, with no more elbow flung up behind him.
- He tears the sword out of the dais, takes it in both hands and holds it upright before
  him, so his mask stays visible while his name shows. He then settles into his guard.
- The pull to the centre of the hall now has weight. He steps into a long throw, winds the
  chain round his forearm, takes it in both fists and sinks back against it before he
  heaves. He lands short of the sword and ploughs to a stop on his heels and one fist,
  stays down a moment, then wrenches the blade out of the floor and rises. The scene is
  3 seconds longer (61 s).
