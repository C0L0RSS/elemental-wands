# A new Guardian intro

- The Guardian no longer wakes from a heart. A zombie wanders into the nave, looks up, and the
  Guardian drops out of the vaults on it with a two-fisted hammer smash.
- It hauls the zombie up by the head, tears it in two, flings the legs away and hurls the rest
  over your heads, then points at you. It moves like the slow, heavy brawler it is.
- The name card and the skip (hold Sneak, everyone) work as before.
