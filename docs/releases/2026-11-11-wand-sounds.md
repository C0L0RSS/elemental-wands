# Every wand has its own sounds

- No spell plays a vanilla Minecraft sound any more. All five elements have their own sound sets,
  141 sounds in all, and each has its own character.
- Fire roars, whooshes and thumps, with sparks only inside the blast.
- Wind is round, controlled air: a soft push and a breathy tone that glides with the movement.
  Gale Daggers ring like thrown blades, and now also make a sound when the three daggers form.
- Stone is the deepest: boulders knocking together, heavy thuds, grinding and crumbling rock.
- Nature is the lightest: small bells, quick runs of notes and twinkling fairy dust. Rising notes
  mean growing or healing; falling notes mean something ending.
- Space inhales, drops into a dark tone and leaves a glassy shimmer.
- Where a spell used to stack two or three sounds at one moment, it now plays one made for that
  moment. Spell sounds also have their own captions, such as "Meteor falls" or "Stone Wall rises".
- Bosses, mobs and cutscenes still use their earlier sounds.
