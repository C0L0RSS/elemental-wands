# Space: Gravity Well

- Throw a compact, pulsing energy cube. Its shell bursts open on impact,
  unfurling a gravity well. Shoot and teleport while enemies fight the pull,
  then cast again to collapse it early.
- The four-second field also collapses automatically, dealing a burst of damage.
  Its 16-second cooldown starts on impact, so early collapse does not reset it.
- A deep-purple vortex, visible perimeter and constant inward particle stream
  show the range.
  Collapse accelerates the stream into the orb for a brief buildup, then explodes
  outward as the damage lands.
- Allies and your double are protected. The Guardian takes burst damage without
  being dragged around.

Unlock Gravity Well for 500 Flux. Space now has five spells, bringing every
current elemental wand to at least five choices for its five free slots.
