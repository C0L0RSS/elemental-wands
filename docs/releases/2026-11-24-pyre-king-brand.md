# Pyre King: the Pyre Brand replaces the spin

- The Pyre King no longer whirls his sword round him on its chain. Nothing of his has to be
  jumped or crouched under now except the chains of his phase change.
- In its place he sets the floor alight. He lowers the point of his sword to the floor,
  rears back over it, and drives it into the stone.
- A fan of the floor in front of the sword then burns for 1.8 seconds, two blocks tall, from
  about 3 to 12 blocks in front of him. It cannot be jumped: get off that floor. It is wider
  in his second phase.
- It is a middle-range move. Within about six blocks he keeps to his sword.
- Low flames show every block that will burn for 1.2 seconds before it does.
- Being on it as it catches costs about what his chain does, and staying to the end about
  one sword blow. A shield does not stop it. A pillar does: the floor behind one stays dark.
- He kneels behind the sword for as long as the floor burns, so anyone off the fan has
  that long to hit him.
