# The graveyard mausoleum

- The Necromancer's graveyard has a gothic mausoleum at its far end in place of the open grave and headstone. Walk through the dark veil in its marble doorway to start the fight; everyone within 16 blocks comes with you, as before.
- Look at the doorway and you're told how to start the fight. Faint motes drift slowly into the veil, and the windows look into the same dark void.
- Nobody falls into a grave any more, and the mausoleum can't be mined, blown up or pushed. After a fight you come back to the court in front of the steps, not into the doorway.
- The woods around a graveyard are dead and dying: bare trees, scarred ground and fallen leaves make a yard easy to spot from a distance.
- Reward chests now appear either side of the mausoleum steps. Graveyards generated earlier keep their headstone, which still works.
