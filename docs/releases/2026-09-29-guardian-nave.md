# 2026-09-29 — The Shattered Nave

- The Church Guardian is now fought in its own realm, the Shattered Nave: an endless ruined
  cathedral of giant broken pillars that fade into haze in every direction, with coloured
  light falling from windows nobody can see.
- Offering the heart at the church takes everyone within 20 blocks straight into the nave.
  A short cinematic opens every fight: the heart trembles in your outstretched hand, flies the
  length of the hall into the kneeling stone Guardian, and it wakes wreathed in lightning, rises
  and slams its fists together. Hold Sneak to skip; it ends once everyone has skipped.
- The fight floor is the same size as before. Nothing you own drops if you fall there: you
  watch the rest of the fight and return with your belongings.
- Win, and you return to find the church already restored. Several churches can now be
  fought at the same time.
- The raised sky floor and its ride up are gone, and so are the old "clear the sky" and
  "gather in the open courtyard" ritual refusals.
