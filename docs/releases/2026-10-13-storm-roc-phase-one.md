# Storm Roc: first combat phase

- The Storm Summit now supports the Roc’s first combat phase: repeated lightning strikes,
  drifting tornadoes, a diving impact and jumpable shockwave, then a stuck opening and ground brawl.
- The giant Roc pecks, sweeps its beak, screams, beats its wings, pounces and stampedes.
  Damage dealt ends each landing; half health ends this phase-only encounter.
- Operators can start and reset attempts on the summit. Defeated players spectate with
  belongings kept, and the group can retry after a wipe. The aerie, cinematics, second
  phase and victory rewards are still to come.
