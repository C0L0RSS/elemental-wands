# Heavier, more readable Necromancer and undead animations

- **The Hollow Necromancer** leans on its staff and hoists it with every soul bolt, which now
  thrusts as each bolt leaves. It stoops and wrenches its fist shut to raise the hands, whirls
  into shadow when it blinks, and drives its staff into the floor to ambush. The colossus
  breathes and shifts its weight, cocks and lands its swipe with a claw slam, rears to spit and
  drink souls, braces its hind feet for the rush, and rears high before it plunges into its dive.
- **The hollow undead** claw up out of the grave and die with real weight. The brute heaves
  its maul overhead and buries it in the ground; the crawler rears and rakes as it lunges; the
  archer draws an arrow from its quiver, nocks and draws to its jaw before it looses.
