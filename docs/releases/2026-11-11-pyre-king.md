# The Pyre King

The Fire shardbearer: the cursed old king of a fallen Nether kingdom, a swordsman with his
greatsword chained to his wrist.

- **The way in.** Somewhere in the Nether a bridge runs out from a lava shore to nowhere. At its
  end stands a lone gate, its arch filled with fire and chained shut, the king's seal hanging on
  the chains. Right-click the seal, then again within five seconds: the chains burn, and you and
  everyone within 16 blocks are taken to his hall. `/locate structure elementalwands:pyre_gate`
  finds one.
- **The Burned Throne Hall.** His roofless throne hall on a crag in a lava sea, lit by twelve
  great braziers. Its stone can't be broken.
- **The intro.** He sits slumped on his throne like a statue, bound in a web of chains, his
  sword hanging above him. The chains burn through, the sword falls, his hand closes on the
  hilt, and the hall takes fire. Hold Sneak to skip.
- **The fight.** Sword strings up close; at range he throws the sword, hooks you in, or hauls
  himself after it on its chain. Hide behind a pillar and he flies round it. His chain swings
  in a circle: jump the low one, crouch under the high one, or step in close under it. At half
  health his chains tear loose and the fight quickens. His fire on the floor is real fire;
  Fire Resistance doesn't stop it.
- **After.** Win, and ten seconds later you stand before the gate again. Fall, and you wake
  there with everything you carried; when nobody is left standing he resets.

Tuned for diamond to netherite armor. No reward and no death animation yet, and his sounds are
placeholders. Played in Lunar by one player; the gate and co-op are not yet playtested.
