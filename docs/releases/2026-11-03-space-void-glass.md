# Space spells in void glass

- All five Space spells are now drawn in 3D "void glass" to match the Fire and Wind looks: dark
  see-through glass with a bright violet frame and stars inside, around black cores ringed by a
  glowing corona. Space falls inward, so streams rush into their cores rather than streaming out.
- Singularity Bolt is a black star with a broken orbit that drags the space behind it into itself.
  A hit swells it, pulls the space around it back in and pinches it shut; a miss folds away at
  full range.
- Blink Rift opens a standing eclipse slit at both ends. You fold into the first as void glass and
  coalesce out of the second as shards spill back out; with no safe spot the slit only flickers.
- Astral Double's orb is an amethyst crystal in a glass cube. The double forms out of the floor's
  glass, wears your skin under a dark starry shell, and has glass plates circling its waist. It
  dissolves in a poof when destroyed, expired or swapped with.
- Gravity Well's bomb is a black cube inside six glass plates. Its field is a floor vortex of large
  glass plates spiralling into a hovering eclipse core inside a segmented ring at the pull radius,
  then a buildup and an outward burst.
- Hollow Purple's wind-up circles a blue and a red star above you as you rise; they merge in a white
  flash into a ten-block sphere of falling glass around a huge black core. It trails a purple
  double helix while rings seal the tunnel behind it, then collapses into a point.
- Damage, ranges and cooldowns are unchanged. Stars and small shards follow your particle setting.
