# Fractured Guardian: a smarter, heavier fight

- The Guardian now chooses its attacks from where you stand and what you are doing, in
  place of a fixed rotation. Up close it slams; as you back off it walks in behind a
  wave or pens you with the Scar; at range it leaps; beyond that it throws rocks.
- It strings attacks together with no pause between them, more often and for longer after
  its shell breaks. In co-op each attack of a string turns on a different player. Slams and
  throws are no longer tripled.
- After a string it walks at you for 1.25 seconds (less with more players) before it attacks
  again. That is the time to hit it.
- Its hits hurt more. The slam's wave and a rock's splash take about a third of your health
  in iron armor, and the Shockwave is now simply the slam, at any range.
- It remembers whether you jumped its last wave. If you did, the next slam hangs overhead
  before it falls.
- The leap follows you into the air before it commits, then marks an amber ring where it
  will land. Sprint out of the ring.
- Eating, drinking, or standing still at range draws an attack at once, usually a rock.
- The fan is gone. Being launched into the air now draws the Airburst: stones that burst
  where you are heading and knock you down. Change direction in the air to beat it.
- In co-op, stones from its back now burst on amber rings under the players it is not
  attacking.
- New: the Sweep. Getting beside or behind it no longer makes you safe. It swings a backhand
  through everything within 7 blocks except the ground straight in front of it, without
  turning first. An amber arc on the floor shows where. Get in front of it or out of reach.
- New: the Scar, which replaces the beam. It drives its hands into the floor and two walls of
  stone spikes erupt either side of you, too tall to jump, for six seconds. They box you in
  rather than hit you; then it leaps or slams into the gap. Run out along the walls, and
  don't touch the spikes. In co-op the rest of the party get a wall each.
