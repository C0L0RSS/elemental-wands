# Springbloom and Updraft landings no longer hurt

- Fixed Springbloom launches sometimes landing with full fall damage, most often in busy
  fights such as the Pyre King's, where a frame hitch could end the protection before you
  had even left the pad.
- Wind's Updraft had the same fault and is fixed the same way.
- You still take every other kind of damage during the flight, and an ordinary fall after
  you have landed hurts as usual.
