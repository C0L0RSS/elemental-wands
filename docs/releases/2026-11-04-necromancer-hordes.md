# Necromancer sieges: hordes and a harder-hitting brute

- Siege waves are hordes now, about two and a half times bigger: 10 to 18 undead a
  wave solo and 14 to 24 with two players, plus more reinforcement crawlers clawing up
  beside you while a wave stands.
- Siege crawlers are much faster (about 4.4 blocks a second). You can't walk away from
  them any more; sprint or kill them.
- The Hunched Brute has a new charging slam: it stomps toward you heaving its maul
  overhead, crouches, then leaps forward and slams it down where you stood when it crouched.
  Step aside late or back off to dodge it. Wild brutes at night fight the same way.
