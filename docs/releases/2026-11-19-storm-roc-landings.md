# Storm Roc: every wand lands its own way

- When the Storm Roc flings you at its summit, your wand now saves you, in its own style:
  - **Fire:** a Flamethrower jet brakes your fall like a thruster, then you drop into a Fire
    Leap that rolls a ring of fire across the snow.
  - **Wind:** you zigzag down through the air on chained Waylay Dashes, the last in slow
    motion, and skim in to a skid.
  - **Stone:** time slows as you tear rock out of the summit and it streams up into a huge
    boulder round yourself; you crash down in it, and stand up as it breaks apart.
  - **Nature:** a Springbloom bounces you sky-high, time slows, and a Thornbite vine swings
    you down into a slide that leaves grass and flowers behind.
  - **Space:** you fall without slowing, blink away just before you hit, and walk out of a
    rift beside the crater, straightening your cuffs.
- You hold your own wand all the way through, and every spell comes out of its tip.
- You wear the armour you have on, dyes, trims and enchantment glint included.
- A party lands side by side in a line facing the Roc, two blocks apart, instead of all on one
  spot. Each of you still sees your own landing; the others are standing in their places when
  it ends. The boulders, bones and feathers that stood where the line falls have gone.
- Every boss now sets a party down side by side, not on one spot: arriving in the Hollow
  Crypt and the Burned Throne Hall, and going home after the fight in front of the Storm Roc's
  gong, the Pyre King's seal and the crypt's courtyard. The Guardian already did.
- Every landing ends looking over your shoulder as you glare up at the Roc coming down onto
  its spire.
- The struggle in its talon is easier to follow: it opens on the whole Roc over the clouds and
  closes in on you fighting it.
- A wizard with no element still crashes into the snow and rolls.
