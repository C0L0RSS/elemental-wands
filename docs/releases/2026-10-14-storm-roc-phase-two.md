# Storm Roc: the storm phase

- At half health the Roc climbs into the sky, the summit goes quiet and a storm rolls in.
  A huge bolt charges it, and the fight goes on under dark clouds, rain and thunder.
- In the storm its lightning is bigger and hits harder. Large strikes leave the ground live
  for a moment, and strikes keep falling while it brawls.
- The tornadoes swell and a third joins them.
- Instead of one dive it chain-smashes: four beak slams in a row, each turned on a player
  and never far from the last.
- Its tornadoes are now chunky, blocky clouds, and its lightning bolts are bolder.
- After the first playtest:
  - the Roc is half the size, with eagle eyes that face forward;
  - every animation is new. On each spire it lands, looks around like a bird and rears up
    to call its lightning down out of the clouds;
  - its head snaps round to watch whoever it is striking, and it banks as it flies;
  - tornadoes are textured. They drag you in, whirl you high and throw you, and they creep
    after you while the Roc is in the air;
  - a thin layer of cloud now hangs over the summit, and phase one's lightning falls from it.
- Defeating it ends the fight for now. Its death, the cinematics and the shard reward are
  still to come. Operators can skip a fight to the storm phase with `/ew summit skip`.
