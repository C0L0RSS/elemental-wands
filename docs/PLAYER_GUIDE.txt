ELEMENTAL WANDS — PLAYER GUIDE
================================


WHAT IS THIS MOD?
-----------------
Elemental Wands puts a powerful magical arsenal in your hands. Instead of swords
and axes, you'll wield wands tied to the fundamental forces of nature — Fire, Ice,
Wind, Stone, and Space — each with a completely unique combat style and three
distinct abilities to master.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
When you first join a world, you'll receive two things automatically:
  - A Fractured Wand — a broken, unawakened wand that can shoot a basic soul-flame beam
  - The Wizard's Path — a guidebook that tracks your progression

The Fractured Wand is just a starting tool. Its secondary and ultimate abilities
are locked with a message: "The core is fractured... find a Crystal to awaken it."
Your first real goal is crafting a proper elemental wand.


PROGRESSION: FROM FRACTURED TO ELEMENTAL
-----------------------------------------

Step 1 — Mine Crystal Ore
Five types of crystal ore generate naturally across the world, each tied to an
element (Fire, Wind, Stone, Ice, Space). Mine them to get raw crystals.

Step 2 — Smelt & Craft
Smelt the raw crystals into refined crystals, then combine a refined crystal with
your Fractured Wand at a crafting table to create your chosen elemental wand.

Step 3 — Deal Damage, Earn Arcane Flux
Every point of damage you deal with a wand earns you Arcane Flux — a persistent
magical energy that stays on your character even through death. This is your
progression currency.

Step 4 — Unlock Abilities
Open The Wizard's Path book to see your current Flux and unlock buttons. Spending
Flux (plus XP levels) unlocks your wand's locked abilities:
  - Secondary ability:  500 Arcane Flux + 15 XP levels
  - Ultimate ability:  1500 Arcane Flux + 30 XP levels

Step 5 — Reset (Optional)
Combine a Reset Rune with your elemental wand to revert it back to a Fractured
Wand — useful if you want to switch elements.


HOW ABILITIES WORK
------------------
Every wand has three abilities mapped to three controls:
  - Right-click             → Primary ability (always available)
  - Shift + Right-click     → Secondary ability (unlockable)
  - X key                   → Ultimate ability (unlockable, requires charge)

The HUD above your hotbar shows three slots — one per ability — with a cooldown
indicator so you always know what's ready.

THE ULTIMATE CHARGE SYSTEM
Ultimates don't run on a timer. They use a charge bar (0–100) stored on your wand.
Every hit you land with any wand ability adds +5 charge. When you hit 100, press X
to unleash your ultimate — the charge resets to zero afterward. No charge, no ultimate.


THE FIVE WANDS
--------------

FIRE WAND
Passive: Permanent Fire Resistance while held. Standing on the fire or magma you
create grants Regeneration II and Speed III.

  Primary   — Inferno Wave
    Launches a fast-moving wave of fire at your target.

  Secondary — Dragon's Pyre
    Exhales a cone of flame up to 40 blocks in front of you, dealing damage,
    setting enemies on fire, and coating the ground with magma and fire for
    5 seconds.

  Ultimate  — Maximum Meteor
    Calls down a devastating meteor from the sky that strikes the block you're
    aiming at with a massive explosion.

---

ICE WAND
The coldest wand — focuses on stacking "Frost" debuffs on enemies to trigger
explosive combos.

  Primary   — Frost-Bite Volley
    Fires a spread of three frost shards. Each hit applies a Frost stack.
    At 3 stacks, the enemy Shatters — dealing bonus damage, clearing their
    stacks, and blasting nearby enemies with an icy AoE burst.

  Secondary — Glacial Gust
    Fires five ice-wave projectiles in a spread fan. They pierce enemies and
    apply slowness. Enemies with 2+ Frost stacks get completely rooted.

  Ultimate  — Blizzard
    Calls a sustained blizzard zone centered on where you're aiming, dealing
    ongoing cold damage to all caught inside.

---

WIND WAND

  Primary   — Vacuum Blades
    Fires two parallel crescent wind blades side by side.

  Secondary — Waylay Dash
    A short-range directional dash with 2 charges that regenerate over time.
    Dashing again within 1.5 seconds of the last dash chains it — each chain
    makes the next dash faster and stronger. Applies Slow Falling after each dash.

  Ultimate  — Zephyr Strike
    Launches you explosively into the air (equips an Elytra on you automatically).
    While airborne you are immune to fall damage and leave swirling wind particle
    rings when diving. When you land, you create a massive shockwave explosion
    scaled to how fast you were falling. Your previous chestplate is restored
    automatically.

---

STONE WAND

  Primary   — Earthen Maw (Tectonic Spikes)
    Summons a rapid wave of stone spikes erupting from the ground in a line ahead
    of you, dealing damage and launching enemies upward. Follows the terrain.

  Secondary — Stone Wall
    Instantly raises a 4x4 stone wall two blocks in front of you. While standing
    near your own wall, you gain Resistance III — use it as cover. The wall
    crumbles after a few seconds.

  Ultimate  — Titan Dome
    Summons a massive stone dome around you, creating a fortified zone.

---

SPACE WAND

  Primary   — Singularity Bolt
    Fires a focused bolt of spatial energy.

  Secondary — Blink
    Teleports you up to 10 blocks in the direction you're looking, leaving a Rift
    at your departure point. If a Rift already exists, activating this again will
    teleport you back to it (swapping positions with the rift).

  Ultimate  — Hollow Purple
    Triggers an immense spatial compression charge — a channeled buildup that
    releases a devastating concentrated burst of void energy.


THE FRACTURED WAND (STARTER)
-----------------------------
If you don't yet have an elemental wand, the Fractured Wand fires a basic soul-fire
beam that deals a small amount of damage. It still generates Arcane Flux, so
fighting with it early helps you get ahead on your unlock costs.


TIPS
----
  - You can hold a wand in your offhand — passive bonuses (like Fire Resistance on
    the Fire Wand) still apply.

  - Arcane Flux persists through death — you never lose your progress.

  - The Wizard's Path book updates live with your current Flux amount. When the
    unlock button turns green, you're ready.

  - Frost stacks from the Ice Wand's primary and secondary both contribute to the
    Shatter threshold — mix your abilities for faster stacks.

  - Wind Wand dashes have a charge recharge that ticks even while the wand is in
    your inventory, so they refill passively over time.
