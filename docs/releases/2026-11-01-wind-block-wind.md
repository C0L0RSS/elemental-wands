# Wind spells in block wind

- All five Wind spells are now drawn in 3D "block wind" to match the new Fire look: see-through
  cloud cubes, chunky speed streaks and stepped crescent blades instead of flat cloud sprites.
- Sky Shear's three blades are stepped crescents that face you as they fly, so they read as
  crescents from your own eyes. Two fainter echoes follow each one like motion blur. A hit bursts
  a cloud and a ring on the target; a miss peels apart into two little clouds at full range.
- Waylay Dash opens a pressure ring behind you and leaves a soft cloud trail that widens into a V,
  with curled swooshes peeling off your shoulders. A chained dash doubles the ring and tints the
  outer cloud pale blue.
- Updraft rolls twenty cloud puffs across the floor and winds a double coil up around your launch.
  Speed lines hang under your feet as you rise, and a small puff marks your safe landing.
- Gale Daggers keep their pearl look but are now forged from a swirl of wind over your head,
  building from the grip to the tip. Each shot leaves crisp cloud rings and bursts a small cloud
  where it hits.
- Zephyr Strike now wears 3D feathered wings. Streams pour off them as you rise, rings tighten
  around you in the dive, and you land in a dome of air with a rolling wall of cloud racing out.
- Damage, ranges and cooldowns are unchanged. Motes and small curls follow your particle setting.
