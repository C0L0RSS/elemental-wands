# Hollow Necromancer redesign

- Rebuilt the mage with a taller, leaner silhouette, layered robes and an opening hood.
- Rebuilt the giant skeleton with an open ribcage, curved spine and spread, jointed claws. It crawls on all fours and rushes players to grab, bite and throw them.
- Replaced the skull-pulling transformation: the mage leans back, the skeleton crawls through its hood, and the empty robe falls behind it and burns away.
- Matched the grab's held-player position to the animated hand.
- Soul bolts now use the custom glowing skull, face their flight direction, and chomp through the air before snapping shut on impact.
- Grasping Hands now raises six animated skeletal claws around each marked player. In phase two, a successful catch triggers the rush, bite and throw; the separate grab-and-slam keeps its teammate rescue mechanic.
- Added the Hollow Crypt, the necromancer's own dimension: a huge clearing ringed by a dark, dead forest under a starless sky and thick fog. Graveyards now generate in the overworld (`/locate structure elementalwands:hollow_graveyard`). Use the headstone to drag everyone nearby into the crypt, where the necromancer rises.
- Nothing is lost in the crypt. Fallen players watch the rest of the fight as spectators; if everyone falls, the boss vanishes and you return to the graveyard to try again. The first victory at a graveyard leaves two chests of bones and treasure beside the grave, and every victor can claim one spell book there.
- New night undead roam the Overworld: packs of Hollow Crawlers, pairs of Risen Archers and the rare Hunched Brute with its spiked maul. They claw out of the ground, burn in daylight and drop bones, arrows, rotten flesh and iron. The Necromancer now raises these instead of spectral zombies and skeletons.
- Life Drain now draws a braided soul stream toward the caster, with drifting mist and ghostly wisps around the drained player’s first-person view.
- Reworked Fire Leap: a shorter 22-block range with a higher arc that scales with distance, smooth flight you control like normal movement (no more sitting pose), a short landing skid, and aiming that shortens the leap to the farthest clear spot instead of refusing.
