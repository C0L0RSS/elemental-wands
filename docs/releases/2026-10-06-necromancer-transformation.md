# The Necromancer grows into the colossus

- Phase two now opens with a 35-second cinematic. The Necromancer drops onto his staff and it
  snaps; the souls trapped inside break loose in a storm, two of them floor him and the rest
  pour into his face.
- On his back, his fingers stretch into claws and every convulsion is a growth spurt that rips
  his robe further, until, inside a burst of smoke, he is the giant. It sits up, its eyes
  ignite, it crawls at you and howls, and the braziers flare back to life.
- Everyone at the fight watches it together and can't be hurt while it plays. Hold Sneak to skip.
