# Fire spells in block fire

- All six Fire spells are now drawn in chunky 3D "block fire" instead of flat flame cards and
  particles: cubes that rise, tumble, cool from white-hot to deep red and shrink away.
- Inferno Wave is a rolling fireball with flames swept back behind it. It sheds glowing pieces
  along its path and splashes against the wall it hits.
- Flamethrower pours a cone of flame cubes that grow and redden toward the 6-block reach and
  spread across walls and the ground. Near overheat it sputters, and on overheating it coughs
  smoke. In your own view the stream thins around the crosshair so you can still aim.
- Dragon's Pyre runs as five tall fire columns that climb steps and drop into dips. They leave
  a carpet of flames a few inches high.
- Fire Leap throws a ring of fire at take-off and trails a comet tail through the air. On
  landing, a knee-high wall of fire rolls out to 5 blocks.
- Flashover coals are charred lumps with glowing cracks that breathe heat once armed. Each one
  bursts into a ball of fire exactly as big as its 4-block blast.
- The Meteor is a stepped ball of block fire with a ring of flames marking where it will land.
  On impact, a dome of fire blows out to its 10-block damage edge.
- Targets that catch fire still burn with Minecraft's own fire. Damage, ranges and cooldowns
  are unchanged. Sparks and smoke follow your particle setting.
