# 2026-09-21 — Stone techniques

- Added Faultline: a broad wave of crumbling stone spikes that briefly interrupts
  enemies, including on leaf canopies and water surfaces.
- Added Juggernaut: hold to build a heavy charge with limited steering, release
  to brake, and jump at full speed for a longer leap.
- Strong charge impacts smash a limited cone of ordinary blocks. Stone is harder
  to break than dirt or wood; containers and protected structures remain intact.
- Both Techniques cost 500 Stone Flux, bringing the element to five spells.
