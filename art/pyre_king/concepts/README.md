# Pyre King concept

**Approved:** [the masked king](direction-d-mask.png).
- A lean duelist body in charred black armor with tarnished gold trim.
- A scorched gold death mask over a long ash beard.
- A heavy gold crown with a red gem, and the Fire shard in his chest.
- Burned crimson mantle scraps, and curse chains with padlocks.
- A longsword chained from his right wrist to the pommel.

**Kept for later:** [the burned king](direction-d-ash.png). It has the same body, but his
own face and beard have turned to ash and slag. It is the candidate for what is revealed
when the mask breaks at the phase change.

The model differs from these sheets where the developer asked: a 4.5-block English
longsword held two-handed, a knee-length coat so the footwork reads, and fire covering
the whole blade, always burning upward. These are concept images, not measured model
sheets. Earlier rounds (three alternative directions, and contact sheets) are kept locally
in `.local-previews/pyre-king/concepts-archive/`.
