# A harder Guardian

- The Guardian hits much harder. Its slams now strike with the fists as well as the ground wave,
  and two or three heavy hits will put you down in iron armor.
- Some slams pause at the top of the swing before they smash, so you can't jump the wave on rhythm.
- The repeating guard break is gone. At half health the stone shell bursts open (back away when it
  starts to crack and grind), and the core stays exposed for the rest of the fight.
- Phase two adds a core pulse: the Guardian drags everyone nearby toward its core, then detonates.
  Sprint away.
- Fighting together is harder too. It rests less between attacks, flings stones at the players it
  isn't facing, and goes after whoever is hurting it most, though never ignores anyone for long.
- Your world's difficulty sets how hard the Guardian fights.
