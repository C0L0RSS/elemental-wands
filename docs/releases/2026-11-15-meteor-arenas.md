# Fire: Meteor works inside boss arenas

- Meteor no longer bursts on the invisible ceiling over the Hollow Crypt and the Burned
  Throne Hall. In every boss arena it now starts under the ceiling and falls onto the
  fight.
- In an arena, aiming at a wall drops the meteor beside the wall, and aiming at a ceiling
  drops it to the floor below, instead of exploding inside the block.
- Outside the arenas nothing changes: a roof or a tree still stops a meteor above it.
