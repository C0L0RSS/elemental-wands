# Natural gray Stone review

`before-after.png` and `contact-sheet.png` show the actual generated game textures.
Armor is shown as a flat projection of the vanilla UV faces. `concept.png` is an
art-direction illustration produced with the built-in imagegen tool; it is not
in-game proof or a promised replacement model.

Production assets are authored at exact game resolution through the existing
editable native source in `tools/generate_stone_vfx_assets.py`. The illustration
was not resized into armor UVs or substituted for the game sprites.

## Built-in imagegen prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one art-direction sheet for a Minecraft
Stone magic equipment and terrain texture redesign. Show a coherent natural gray
stone family: on the left three jagged blocky rock spikes and a small stacked stone
wall; in the middle a dome made of large stone blocks; on the right a Minecraft
humanoid wearing a complete suit of carved stone plate armor holding a broad
chipped stone sword. Crisp authentic Minecraft pixel-textured cuboids, readable
medium-light cool neutral gray stone, restrained layered stone grain, beveled
chipped edges, pale gray mineral flecks with only tiny desaturated sage hints.
Armor must read as substantial coherent plates with dark gray joints, clean
helmet brow, chest breastplate, stone shoulder caps, knee plates, boots. Sword
looks like a honed slab of rock with chipped gray edge and charcoal leather grip.
No gold, no brown, no orange, no glowing cracks, no glossy metal, no pure black
heavy outlines, no noisy camouflage. Flat studio charcoal background, even neutral
daylight. A professional game asset concept board showing one consistent kit,
not a gameplay screenshot. No text.
