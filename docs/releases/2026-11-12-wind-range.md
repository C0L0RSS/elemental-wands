# Wind: longer-reaching Sky Shear

- Sky Shear now reaches 20 blocks, up from seven, so you can keep attacking while
  repositioning in boss fights.
- Its damage falls more gently over that longer distance: seven up close, 5.5 at
  ten blocks and four at maximum range.
- The three-blade fan still hits each enemy once per cast, stops at solid cover
  and keeps its one-second recovery.
