# Storm Roc: the aerie

- The Storm Roc can now be found in survival. On the summits of jagged, frozen and stony
  peaks stands an abandoned Windcaller gong: a round bronze gong in a weathered dark oak frame,
  a wrapped mallet leaning on a post, and the bones and feathers of those who came before. The
  summit around it is the mountain's own snow or stone. `/locate structure
  elementalwands:storm_aerie` points to one.
- Strike the gong once to hear who would go, and again within five seconds to call the Roc.
  You and your party members within 24 blocks are carried up to the summit after three strikes,
  and the Roc attacks a few seconds after you land.
- Lose, and the wind sets you back down at the gong to try again, with your belongings kept.
- Win, and after a moment on the quiet summit you return to find a reward chest at the gong's
  foot. Every challenger, including any who fell, can claim one Ultimate spell book there
  (a free Basic, Technique or Ultimate spell), once per aerie.
- The Roc has no death animation yet, and the snatch-and-throw intro is still to come.
