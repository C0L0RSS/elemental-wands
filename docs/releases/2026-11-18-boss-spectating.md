# Spectating boss fights

- If you fall in a Pyre King arena fight, respawn to watch your teammates from above the hall
  with your items and XP. The hall sends the party home in their original game modes after a
  win or a wipe. This also works when an operator starts the fight in the hall.
- Fallen teammates can now see the boss's health bar in the Pyre King, Hollow Necromancer
  and Storm Roc fights, just as they can in the Fractured Guardian fight.
