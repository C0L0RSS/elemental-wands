# Hollow Necromancer: a smarter fight

- The Necromancer now answers what you do. Stand still or eat and he appears behind you;
  hide and his hands come up under you or he moves to where he can see you; stand together
  and the hands come for both of you. He drains when he has been hurt, not at random.
- He teleports less. He no longer moves on a count: he shifts to see round cover, to catch
  up with you, or to get out from under heavy fire, and casts as soon as he lands.
- New attack, the Soul Burst. Get on top of him once and he blinks away. Chase him down
  again and he stands his ground and slams his staff: step back out of the ring, then punish
  the second he stands still.
- Grasping Hands now rise where you are heading. Turn away; do not run on.
- He strings casts together, more in his second duel, and more again with each extra
  player. In a party, a string turns on someone else.
- The giant skeleton reads you too. Jump its swipe and its hand comes for you next; back
  away and it charges; stand still at range and it dives under you. It harvests souls when
  it is wounded, not on a timer.
- The flying head is gone. At a quarter health the giant now rears, howls and goes into a
  frenzy: it crawls faster, barely rests, strings its attacks and stops healing.
- The siege hordes hit harder: crawlers, brutes and archers all deal more damage.
