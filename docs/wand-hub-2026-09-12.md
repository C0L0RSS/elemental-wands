# Native wand hub — September 12, 2026

The approved parchment preview is now a native Minecraft menu. Press **H** or
use **`/ew hub`**. A new player chooses an affinity once; an existing player's hub
shows only their current affinity. The Fire corner burns, Stone has a rock
paperweight, Nature has vines, Space has stars, and Wind has curled paper/gusts.
The paper and corner exports share one texel per logical GUI pixel. The physical
corner follow-up removes real paper pixels for Fire/Space erosion and Nature/Wind
tears. Fire uses charred fibers and small ember-lit flames; Space leaves violet
dust and partially dissolved paper fragments. Nature's vines are composited behind
the torn sheet, with folded lips covering the stems before they emerge on top.
Wind has lifted, frayed paper strips. Stone leaves the sheet intact and adds shaded
creases beneath the contact shadow of its paperweight. Minecraft
supplies the font, buttons, keyboard navigation and existing spell icons.

## Controls and loadouts

- Defaults: **left mouse** casts slot 1, **right mouse** slot 2, **X** the ultimate.
- Rebind these slots in the hub's **Controls** tab. Select a binding then press a
  keyboard key or click a mouse button. Escape cancels capture. Duplicate spell
  bindings swap; the hub's current key is reserved. Defaults can be restored.
- **H** is separately rebindable in Minecraft's normal Controls screen, accessible
  through a button in the hub. `/ew hub` remains available if a binding conflicts.
- Hold the basic attack to repeat it. Techniques and ultimates activate on press.
- **Sneak + right mouse** performs normal block interaction with a held wand.
  Putting the wand away restores vanilla mouse/keyboard behavior.
- Two regular slots and one ultimate are saved per player and affinity. Equipping
  a regular spell in the other regular slot swaps the two without duplicating it.
- Casting or dealing/taking damage locks loadout changes for ten seconds. Arena
  participation locks changes for the encounter. The hub itself can remain open
  during combat and does **not pause** the world.
- Swapping retains spell cooldown timestamps, special charges, Stone reserve and
  ultimate charge. Cooldowns follow spells rather than their assigned inputs.
- Only the existing spells are included. The stable spell catalog/loadout layer
  supports expanding the collection later; new spells and upgrades are deferred.

## Existing saves and progression

Absent loadout data resolves to each affinity's current primary, secondary and
ultimate. Existing affinity, Arcane Flux, unlock bits and wand NBT are preserved.
The new attachment persists and copies on death. Spell bindings are client-local
in `config/elementalwands-controls.properties`; an existing registered ultimate
binding is migrated from `options.txt` when no new controls file exists.

Existing secondary unlocks still cost **500 Flux + 15 XP levels**; ultimates cost
**1500 Flux + 30 XP levels**. The hub calls the existing server resource checks.
Commands remain available, including the operator-only admin unlock helpers.
Normal unlock/affinity commands also obey the combat lock. Affinity reset still
resets skills and Flux; per-affinity loadout records retain their selections but
cannot bypass freshly locked spells.

New players no longer receive a Wizard's Path book. Old books already in saved
inventories are left alone and remain soulbound; they are no longer regenerated.
The initial hub replaces their onboarding role.

## Implementation

- `data/WandSpells`: stable IDs, element/type/name/description, compatible slot rules.
- `data/EWAttachments.WAND_LOADOUTS`: persistent per-affinity spell selections.
- `util/WandLoadouts`: server gates, equip and cast dispatch, combat tracking.
- `network/ModNetworking`: slot requests, hub actions/results and progression sync.
- `client/WandControls`: local bindings, raw input handling and hold lifecycle.
- `client/screen/WandHubScreen`: adaptive native parchment screen.
- `client/overlay/WandHudOverlay`: selected spell order, cooldowns and binding labels.
- Client-only keyboard/mouse mixins consume bound wand input before vanilla actions.
  The common player interaction hook allows deliberate sneaking block interaction.

All cast requests validate player life/spectator state, held wand, saved spell,
unlocks, arena admission and Hollow Purple commitment on the server. Hub mutations
check the expected affinity, so stale screens cannot change a different affinity.
Cooldown/charge checks continue through the existing handlers. Do not replace them
with client validation. Multiplayer clients and servers must use the same new JAR
because the player-state payload now includes loadout/progression data.

## Artwork

`art/wand_hub/approved-preview.html` preserves the approved interactive design and
corner source. `parchment-source.png` is the approved image-generation texture.
`tools/prepare_wand_hub_assets.py` exports eleven separate UI assets under
`textures/gui/hub/`; it does not change existing spell/VFX PNG contracts.

The prior built-in image-generation prompt requested a finer pixel grid, warm
ivory parchment, restrained fibers/creases and worn margins with a readable blank
center. The exporter performs deterministic grid sampling and perimeter alpha,
and extracts the approved code-authored stone. `tools/wand_hub_corner_art.py`
defines the revised damage masks, torn paper lips, vines behind the sheet, erosion
and creases. Per-affinity parchment textures bake the actual holes; the corner
layer supplies loose particles, lifted fibers and the rock. The exporter verifies
real alpha removal for damaged elements, intact Stone paper, and unchanged pixels
outside the upper-left 64-by-64 region.
