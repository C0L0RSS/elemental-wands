# Fire Leap — September 13, 2026

Fire Leap replaces Fire Hop as an optional Fire Technique. The stable `fire_hop`
spell ID and `hop_ready` cooldown key remain unchanged, preserving purchases,
loadouts, price (500 Fire Flux), and cooldowns. Flashover is deferred.

## Player behavior

Hold the Technique binding to preview a trajectory and landing circle; release
on valid ground to launch. Changing items, opening a screen, or losing focus
cancels aiming. The trajectory is private client geometry. After commitment,
all nearby clients see the destination and five-block warning circle.

Initial tuning: 12 horizontal blocks, up to four blocks higher/six lower,
4.5 blocks of extra arc height, 20 ticks of flight, 120 ticks of cooldown.
Standing and normal sprint jumps (support within 1.5 blocks) work. High airborne
recasts, gliding, vehicles and water are excluded. Invalid geometry costs no cooldown.
The caster can look around but cannot steer, dismount, or cast another spell during
flight. Putting the wand away after takeoff does not cancel the commitment.

Landing produces an expanding fire crest, covering five blocks at .65 blocks/tick.
Its damage band is .6 blocks high, allowing a timed jump. A target takes one hit:
8 damage inside 1.5 blocks of the center, 4 farther out. No takeoff damage,
block destruction, ground fire, or additional ignition damage. Terrain sampling
and cover match the Guardian wave rules. Party members, teammates and allied pets
are protected. Damage earns Fire Flux even if another affinity is selected later.

## Implementation

- `FireLeapRules`: common client/server targeting, whole-body clearance and arc.
- `FireLeapManager`: validates release destinations, ownership and cooldown;
  applies covered, jumpable, once-per-target damage.
- `FireLeapEntity`: invisible committed passenger carrier; both client and server
  sample the same arc. Checks each movement segment for new obstacles. A blocked
  route aborts at the previous clear point without a remote impact or refund.
- `FireLeapPreview`: private trajectory and terrain-following public landing rings.
- `WandControls`: held Technique preview and release payload; cancellation paths.

The carrier uses the existing moving-floor passenger approach to avoid repeated
player teleport corrections. Ordinary landing clears flight fall distance.
The player receives no persistent gravity or invulnerability changes. Death,
disconnect, world changes, removed support and invalid encounter state abort.
Loaded carriers discard themselves instead of resuming their damage or movement.
Minecraft requires a saveable entity type for passengers; this is why reload
cleanup is used rather than disabling the entity type's saving flag.

Existing approved Fire particles are reused. No raster assets are changed.
