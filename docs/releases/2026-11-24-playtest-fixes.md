# Wand controls, boss fights and reward recovery

- Clear a spell slot in the hub to free its binding for ordinary Minecraft actions, such as raising an off-hand shield. Boss cinematics also pause held and alternate casts.
- Hollow Purple leaves dropped items and vehicles alone and respects block protection. Full Bloom still blesses the party where planting is restricted. Titan Dome keeps its borrowed sword and armor with the caster.
- The Guardian clears its boss bar when unloaded. An abandoned Necromancer fight clears its pending transformation, and a Sentry Bloom seed leaves its owner's next hit on the Pyre King available.
- After a Guardian victory, players who stood where a wall was rebuilt return to safe ground. The Nave and Crypt save the trip home before clearing the return record. If a reward chest disappears, an eligible victor can use the spot where it stood to collect their book.
- Switching wands keeps your spell cooldowns. Hits earn Flux and hit-based ultimate charge when they take health from the target. Blink Rift stops where your whole body can fit.
- Temporary spell blocks placed with this version restore the original terrain after an interrupted session. Parties support Floodgate players, and a damaged party save can be repaired while the rest of the server stays playable.
- Fire Leap, Thornbite and Flamethrower do less repeated work while rendering. Block updates near churches use a chunk index.
