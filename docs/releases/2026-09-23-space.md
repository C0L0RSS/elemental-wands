# Space: Astral Double

- Throw a small spatial orb to place a fragile double wearing your skin and a
  violet shimmer. It mirrors your Singularity Bolts for up to 45 seconds.
- Cast again to teleport to the double and consume it. Combine the return with
  Blink Rift for chained repositioning.
- One hostile hit destroys the double, including Guardian attacks. Recovery
  starts when it ends: 15 seconds normally, or three for a failed landing.

Space now has four learnable spells; its fifth remains to be designed.
