# Storm Roc: a new look

- The Roc is rebuilt from solid, chunky blocks with a detailed pixel texture, like a big
  modded boss, in place of the old thousand-cube study.
- It now looks like a bearded vulture: a rust neck and breast, slate wings and back with
  pale feather shafts, rust feathered legs and a long wedge tail.
- Its face is a demon's: steep brows over glowing red slit eyes set deep in a dark mask,
  with spikes sweeping back from the brows.
- A spiky rust crest rests half raised. It flares when the Roc screams, calls its lightning
  or winds up a blow, ruffles when it shakes itself on a spire, and lies flat in flight.
- Its legs are much thicker, ending in huge black sickle talons that bite into the rock and
  curl over a spire's edge to grip it.
- Its tail lifts clear of the rock while it stands, instead of digging into the ground.
