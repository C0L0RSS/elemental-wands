# Hollow Necromancer fight redesign

- The robed Necromancer now fights alone and is hard to pin down: it blinks across the clearing every few spells (a soul flare shows where it will appear) and sometimes appears right behind a player for a heavy soul burst. Keep moving to escape it.
- Grasping Hands covers a much wider ring and hits harder, and a caught player is ambushed at once. Teammates can interrupt the ambush's windup by hitting the Necromancer.
- At three-quarters and half health it retreats to a bough high above the clearing and raises waves of Hollow undead, each harder than the last and sized for the party. Clear them to bring it down: after the first siege it lies stunned and takes extra damage; after the second it tears free as the giant skeleton.
- Every soul bolt that bites now deals its damage.
- The giant skeleton rears and scrapes before its rush, with a glowing lane showing its path, and its rush bite, swipe and grab hit much harder. It no longer raises an army.
- The crypt is brighter, with soul lanterns among the graves and a stronger glow on both forms of the Necromancer.
