# The final boss: the End and its two phases

The first part of the final boss, the First Wizard (a working name): a pool of living ink
over the End that fights as a copy of you.

- **The End is its arena.** There is no Ender Dragon fight any more: no dragon, no crystals,
  no vanilla exit portal. The middle of the main island is rebuilt as a wide round floor
  ringed by broken obsidian pillars. Its stone can't be broken, and no bucket poured on it,
  while the boss is undefeated.
- **Existing worlds keep what players built.** If chests, signs or other placed blocks of
  that kind stand on the main island, the arena is not built and the portal stays shut
  until an operator clears them or runs `/ew end layout force`, which breaks them so their
  contents drop. A dragon egg is moved beside the arrival, never deleted. Players living
  out on the End's other islands are left alone.
- **The pool over the island.** Until the boss is beaten, a pool of living ink hangs over
  the island whenever no fight is running.
- **Entering starts the fight with a cinematic.** Step through an End portal and you arrive
  on the floor's south edge. The first time in a world the Ender Dragon circles under the
  pool, breathes into it, and the pool sends its breath back and kills it; then one edge of
  the pool pours down in front of you and forms into a copy of the first player in, boots
  first, while the sky drains. Later attempts show only the pour. Hold Sneak to skip. While a fight runs,
  other players are refused at the portal; members of the fighting party can still come
  through and join, and the boss grows to match. A refused player is set down beside the
  portal at once, never left standing in it over the stronghold's lava. Mounts, boats and
  thrown ender pearls don't go through, and the boss does not fight on Peaceful.
- **The copy.** It is your size, wears your skin, holds a wand, and ink floats off it. It keeps its distance and casts
  spells from all five elements, each about two to three times the size of yours. Every
  spell forms in the open before it is released and cools into purple and black ink.
- **Its ten spells.**
  - Fireball: swells over its head, then flies slowly. Sidestep it; it leaves burning ground.
  - Meteor: a wide marked circle and a long fall. Leave the circle.
  - Sky Shear: three wide crescents along the floor. Jump each one.
  - Waylay Dash: it crosses the arena along a marked line and leaves a wake that burns.
  - Stone Wall: a tall wall rises behind you and crumbles a few seconds later.
  - Faultline: a marked wave that throws you into the air. Sidestep it; a jump won't clear it.
  - Thornbite: a giant flytrap lunges at you. If it bites, the boss heals.
  - Sentry Bloom: a tall flower shoots at you and stores health for the boss. Break it
    before it delivers.
  - Blink Rift: ink pools where it will appear, anywhere on the floor.
  - Gravity Well: a wide pull, then a burst in the middle. Sprint or dash out.
- **Phase two, at half health.** The fight pauses for a cinematic of about 17 seconds:
  the copy's left arm is wrenched and tears off, falls to the floor and melts into ink; its
  left side swells in slow surges while its head bends the other way, much too far; the
  swelling bursts and three tentacles of ink come out of it, and it lifts off the floor.
  Nobody can be hurt while it plays, and anything phase one left burning is gone. Hold Sneak
  to skip it. Then one tentacle sweeps a full circle round it along the floor: jump it. Its
  head stays bent, and its wand is in its right hand, whichever hand you use. From then it floats
  after you, the tentacles winding round it like snakes and working in every attack, a
  violet ripple running up one before each strike.
  - Slam: a line is marked toward you, then the tendril comes down on it. Step aside.
  - Sweep: a low swing across the floor in front of it. Jump it.
  - Leap: its tentacles plant and throw it into the air; a circle marks where it lands, and
    they slap down round it. Leave the circle.
  - Flame: after a leap it often roots itself and breathes fire from its hand. Get behind it.
    The cloud the flame leaves can be bottled as dragon's breath.
  - Hollow Purple: low on health, it braces and forms your Hollow Purple, bigger, while a lane
    is marked across the arena. Leave the lane.
- **Falling and winning.** Fall, and you wake beside the portal you used with everything you
  carried; when nobody is left standing it resets. Win, and a way home opens at the centre
  of the floor with four gateways to the outer islands.

Not built yet: the intro in which it kills the dragon, rewards, a death

not in the game yet, so it is open to everyone. Tuned for diamond to netherite armor.
Checked by automated fixtures only: nobody has played it in Lunar.
