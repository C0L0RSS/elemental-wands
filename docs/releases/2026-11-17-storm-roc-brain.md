# Storm Roc: a hunter's brain

- The Roc now chooses what to do from where you stand and what you are doing, in the air and
  on the ground, in place of a fixed routine.
- **It punishes straight lines.** Its bite, scream and Raptor Strike aim ahead of you as you
  run and lock about a second before they hit, with a sharp crack. Keep running straight, or
  just stop, and you are hit. Turn sharply when you hear the lock and you are clear.
- Each lock is marked on the rock: a ring of sparks under the bite, sparks along the scream's
  line, and the Roc's shadow where its Raptor Strike will land.
- **Its body turns slowly.** Get beside or behind it and its bill cannot reach you until it
  comes round. There it answers with the Thunderclap: back off, or jump the ring.
- It strings attacks together, more often and for longer in the storm phase, then rests for
  1.25 seconds (less with more players). That is the time to hit it.
- Eating, drinking, a dash or standing still in front of it draws an attack at once. If you
  jump its rings, expect the chest-high sweep. If you keep away, expect the Thunder Charge.
- **Three calls from its spire,** each with its own caw:
  - Volley: a strike where you stand. Keep moving.
  - Fork: three strikes along your path. Turn aside.
  - Cage: a ring of strikes round you. Stand still in the middle.
- **New: the low pass.** It screams, marks a lane of circles across the whole summit and
  flies it low and fast, lightning from its bill walking the lane ahead of it. Step out of
  the lane, then shoot it as it crosses: it is in reach the whole way.
- It hops to the spire nearest you, and decides for itself when to come down.
- **The dive lands on you now,** not on the middle of the summit. Its circle is a little
  smaller: sprint out the moment it appears. Lightning no longer hunts you while it falls.
- The storm phase's chain smash shows each next circle sooner, so a sprint gets out of every
  one.
- Operators: `/ew summit log on` records a fight for tuning.
